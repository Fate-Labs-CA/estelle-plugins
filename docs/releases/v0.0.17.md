# v0.0.17

## Sessions know who they are on Estelle, and messages reach a running session

Every session now gets its Estelle id and session token at every start, including after a compaction, so a message it
sends is marked verified. A message from another of your own sessions can be acted on; a message from a teammate's
session, or from an unverified sender, is shown to you first and waits for your yes.

Messages and room entries now reach a session at its next tool call, not only when you type, at most once every ten
seconds per session and never from a subagent's tool call. The sender can read a receipt: delivered, acknowledged,
answered.

Needs the Estelle server from Fate-Labs-CA/estelle#1878 (live on api.fatelabs.ca). Restart or `/compact` a running
session to give it its identity.

## Limits

- There is still no push into a model mid-reply: a session that is thinking without calling tools, or idle, gets its
  mail at its next tool call or prompt.
- Delivery is at most once: mail is marked delivered when it is handed to the hook.
